# Inter font assets

The three unmodified Inter TTF files were copied from the user-supplied Aguinha reference repository's assets/fonts folder. They allow Courtly mockups and frontend styling to continue without that checkout. Keep the accompanying SIL Open Font License when redistributing the fonts.

Upstream: https://github.com/rsms/inter
License reference: https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/inter/OFL.txt

Inter.ttf is the regular face, InterSemiBold.ttf is semibold, and InterBold.ttf is bold. D4.1 loads these local files through `@font-face` with weights 400, 600 and 700 and `font-display: swap`.
